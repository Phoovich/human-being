# Mirror, not recommender

ไพ่ส่องใจ never scores, ranks, recommends or calls an AI model; it only lays a Student's Options side by side against the Student's own words. Informants asked for comparison (R1, R3, R4, R6, R7), but what weighed on them was comparing *themselves* with others (R2, R3, R5), and they wanted to make the call themselves (R1, R2, R4). A score, a "best path" or an AI suggestion would take that voice away, and R4 already distrusts unfiltered AI output ("ต้องคอยกลับมาวิเคราะห์ รีเช็คข้อมูล").

## Considered Options

- **Weighted comparison table.** What informants literally asked for, but it is the conventional technical fix the brief warns against, and a total per Option is a recommendation in disguise.
- **Seniors' stories library.** Strongest on privilege (R5 has no one to ask), but we have no seniors' stories, so we would have had to invent content.
- **AI follow-up questions.** Livelier, but the questions would no longer come from Informants' words, and it needs a network and an API key for a localhost-only demo.

## Consequences

The design does not fill information gaps (R7's incomplete elective site, R1's wish for neutral facts about pay and pressure), and it does not change who has money or connections. We say so in the talk instead of hiding it.
