# Human Being: Deep Seeing Design Project

A course project that turns what we learned from real students facing a decision into a technology design. It is judged on how deeply we see the people behind the design, not on how finished the technology is.

## Language

### People

**Target Group** (กลุ่มเป้าหมาย):
University students (นิสิต) aged 18–22 who are in the middle of making a decision. Assigned by the instructor.
_Avoid_: users, customers, market

**Informant** (ผู้ให้ข้อมูล):
One of the seven real people from the Target Group who gave us their time and story, referred to only by code R1–R7.
_Avoid_: user, respondent, sample

**Student** (นิสิต):
A member of the Target Group using our design to think through their own Decision.
_Avoid_: user, customer

**Team** (ทีม):
The six of us designing this project.
_Avoid_: นิสิต (the brief uses it for us, but here it always means the Target Group)

### Deciding

**Decision** (การตัดสินใจ):
What a Student or Informant is currently deciding about their own path, such as an elective, an internship, switching faculty, a master's degree or a career track.
_Avoid_: problem, pain point

**Option** (ทางเลือก):
One of the paths a Student is choosing between within a Decision.
_Avoid_: alternative, choice

### The design

**ไพ่ส่องใจ**:
Our design: a card ritual that takes a Student from the tip of their Decision down to its base, then shows them the Mirror. Tagline: "ไม่ทำนาย ไม่ตัดสิน แค่ถาม".
_Avoid_: ไพ่ถามใจ (earlier name), tarot app, fortune-telling, product

**Reading** (การส่องใจหนึ่งครั้ง):
One pass through ไพ่ส่องใจ for one Decision, from naming the Options to the Mirror.
_Avoid_: session, attempt, test, quiz

**Layer** (ชั้น):
One of the three depths of the Iceberg a Reading passes through: the Tip (ยอดน้ำแข็ง: what you see), Beneath (ใต้น้ำ: what you feel) and the Base (ฐาน: what you value). The Student draws one Card from each.
_Avoid_: level, step, stage

**Card** (ไพ่):
One question a Student draws face-down and answers once per Option, written from what Informants said or did.
_Avoid_: prompt, quiz, question item

**Put Back** (วางคืน):
Returning a drawn Card unanswered, without giving a reason: the same right Informants had in our conversations.
_Avoid_: skip, reject

**Echo** (เสียงสะท้อน):
A one-line feeling an Informant shared, reworded so no one can be recognized, shown on a Card so the Student knows others have felt it too. Never a choice or an outcome.
_Avoid_: quote, testimonial, tip

**Mirror** (กระจก):
The closing view that lays a Student's Options side by side against the Student's own answers. It never scores, ranks or recommends.
_Avoid_: result, recommendation, report
